Flint Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Flint is an iPhone email app. Your email stays between your iPhone and your mail provider, except in the case described under Sharing.

Data Flint accesses

With permission from the signed-in person, Flint reads, sends, and organizes that person's email, including messages, senders, recipients, labels, and folders. It connects directly to Google for Gmail and to Apple for iCloud Mail.

Where data is stored

For each email in the inbox and each pinned email, Flint stores the sender, recipients, subject, preview, date, and status on the iPhone, along with app settings. Full messages and attachments are downloaded when opened and aren't saved by Flint. Sign-in credentials are stored in the iPhone's Keychain. Flint doesn't collect analytics. To show new mail alerts, Flint checks for mail from the iPhone in the background when iOS allows it, and those alerts are created on the iPhone.

Sharing

Flint doesn't sell email data or use it for advertising. Email data is shared in one case only: if the person adds their own API key for an AI service, such as Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini, and taps Summarize with AI or Draft with AI, that conversation is sent to the service they chose to produce the result. Separately, Flint loads images hosted online when an email is opened, unless this is turned off in Settings, so the servers hosting them can see that the email was opened and from what network address.

Tips

Tips and the monthly subscription are handled by Apple through the App Store. Flint doesn't receive payment details. It only learns whether a purchase went through and whether a subscription is active.

Google user data

Flint's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Removing an account in Flint's Settings deletes its sign-in credentials and stored messages from the iPhone. Google access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing Flint doesn't delete email from the Gmail or iCloud account.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on Flint's Google sign-in screen.
