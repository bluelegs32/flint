Flint Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Flint is a personal iPhone email app built for its developer's own use. It isn't available to download or offered as a service.

Data Flint accesses

With permission from the signed-in person, Flint reads, sends, and organizes that person's email, including messages, senders, recipients, labels, and folders. It connects directly to Google for Gmail and to Apple for iCloud Mail.

Where data is stored

Recent message details and app settings are stored on the iPhone. Sign-in credentials are stored in the iPhone's Keychain. Flint has no server of its own and doesn't collect analytics.

Sharing

Flint doesn't sell email data or use it for advertising. Email content is shared in one case only: if the person adds their own Claude API key and taps Summarize with AI or Draft with AI, that conversation is sent to Anthropic to produce the result.

Google user data

Flint's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Removing an account in Flint's Settings deletes its sign-in credentials and stored messages from the iPhone. Google access can also be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing Flint doesn't delete email from the Gmail or iCloud account.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on Flint's Google sign-in screen.
